How Alvuna works

Alvuna has two parts: an integration you install in your Home Assistant and an app on the TV. The TV reads the state of your home (rooms, devices, weather, camera images) directly from your Home Assistant and sends it commands, such as turning on a light. None of this passes through a server of ours.

What data is processed, and where

  • On the TV: the addresses of the Home Assistants the TV is paired with, the names you gave them, access tokens and look settings (wallpaper, tiles, screensaver). They stay in the app's storage on the TV until you remove the app or sign out.
  • In your Home Assistant: when you pair a TV, the integration creates a separate user for it (without admin rights) and stores your layout adjustments (hidden items, names, order). You control these as the administrator of your Home Assistant.
  • On your local network: the app looks for Home Assistant on your network so you don't have to type an address with the remote: it listens for its announcement (mDNS), asks for homeassistant.local and, if that finds nothing, checks port 8123 on the addresses of the TV's own subnet. These requests stay inside your network.
  • Sample home: "Try a sample home" opens the demo from this website (alvuna.shipapp.pl) with made-up devices. It does not connect to your Home Assistant and sends nothing about it.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal data or data about your home.
  • The app has no analytics, no crash reports sent to us, no ads and no tracking tools.
  • We don't sell or share data, because we have none.
  • The TV never learns your Home Assistant password: pairing uses a code that you confirm yourself.

Other companies' services

Alvuna uses no third-party services. If you connect the TV to Home Assistant through remote access (for example Home Assistant Cloud or your own proxy), that traffic goes through that service on its provider's terms. You get the app from your TV maker's store, which processes data under its own policy.

Deleting data

  • On the TV: Settings → Connection → Sign out, or remove the app.
  • In Home Assistant: deleting the Alvuna TV device removes the TV's user and its tokens. Removing the Alvuna integration also deletes the layout adjustments.
